SECE offers an array of industry-aligned Undergraduate (B.E. / B.Tech), Postgraduate (M.E.), and Ph.D. research programmes. The core technical domains offered include:

  • Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
  • Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS)
  • CSE - Cyber Security / AI & ML
  • Computer and Communication Engineering (CCE)
  • Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)
  • Information Technology (IT)
  • Electronics Engineering(VLSI Design And Technology)

Extra activities:

🎭Cultural & Fine Arts Clubs

Students regular perform at college festivals and inter-collegiate events through targeted clubs:
• Groovex (Dance Club): Focuses on classical, western, and contemporary dance choreography, representing the college in major competitions.
• Harmonix (Music Club): Connects vocalists and instrumentalists. Members utilize the college's dedicated sound recording studio to mix tracks and record podcasts.
• Dramatics Club: A platform for acting, street plays, and mime art.
• Fashion Club: Organizes runway shows and styling workshops for campus events.

🗣️Media, Language & Soft Skills

• Toastmasters Club: Helps students master public speaking, deliver professional presentations, and sharpen interpersonal communication.
• Media Max: Manages event logistics, campus photography, and digital social media branding for institutional activities.
• Movie Club: Hosts regular film screenings "under the stars" alongside cinema quiz competitions.
• Tamil Mandram: Promotes regional literature, traditional heritage, debating, and creative Tamil writing.

🎖️ National Cadet Corps (NCC) & Social Outreach

• NCC Wing: Operates a highly disciplined Tri-Services military training wing (Army, Navy, and Air Force) under the 2 TN CTC NCC unit, focusing on drills and small arms handling.
• SESSWA & NSS: Organizes essential community service initiatives, blood donation drives, village adoptions under the Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) framework, and plastic awareness cleanups.

Sports

The institution provides extensive facilities spanning multiple acres to accommodate diverse sporting interests:
• Outdoor Grounds: Dedicated playing fields for Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Basketball, Hockey, Kabaddi, and Ball Badminton.
• Athletics Track & Field: Setups optimized for track events (100m to 10,000m runs) and field disciplines such as Javelin, Shot Put, Long Jump, High Jump, and Triple Jump.
• Indoor Sports: Fully operational spaces housed within the campus Amenity Centre catering to Table Tennis, Carrom, and Chess.
• Fitness Facility: A modern, well-equipped gymnasium accessible to both hostellers and day scholars to support physical fitness.
