Undergraduate (B.E. / B.Tech.): Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), CSE (AI & ML), Computer and Communication Engineering (CCE), CSE (Cyber Security), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), Mechanical Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI & DS), and Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS). Postgraduate (M.E.): VLSI Design, CSE, Engineering Design. Doctoral Programs: Ph.D. programs approved by Anna University across engineering, science, and humanities.

Sri Eshwar College of Engineering (SECE) is a prominent private autonomous engineering college located in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. Established in 2008 by the Eshwar Educational and Charitable Trust, the institution has built a strong reputation for academic excellence, holistic education, and stellar corporate placements
